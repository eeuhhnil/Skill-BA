# Market Research Pack

Serves: enter or skip a market, position a product, pick a segment, pitch investors.

**Dimensions (priority order — prune to the decision):**

1. Market size & growth — the *reachable* market, not the headline TAM
2. Customer segments & behavior — who buys, deciding how, valuing what
3. Pain points & unmet needs — what they complain about, work around, go out of their way to avoid
4. Competitive landscape — who competes for these customers, including substitutes and "do nothing"
5. GTM dynamics — channels, sales motion

**Craft (the non-obvious):** 1–3★ reviews are the gold for pains; public-company 10-K/S-1 industry sections are free analyst-grade sizing; read competitors' own product pages and docs directly, never roundups; funding history + job postings reveal competitor trajectory; a complaint pattern persisting across years is a stronger finding, not a stale one.

**Freshness:** size/growth ≤ 18 mo · feature claims ≤ 3 mo · behavior data ≤ 2 yr · GTM benchmarks ≤ 12 mo.

**Two-source classes:** market size and growth figures; any quantitative claim a recommendation rests on; competitor traction claims.

**Feeds (bmm):** brief (opportunity, problem, users) · PRD (personas, differentiation) · GTM decisions.

**Out of scope:** pricing, plans/tiers, costs, and anything about giá — never research, tabulate, or report them in this pack, even as a side finding.
