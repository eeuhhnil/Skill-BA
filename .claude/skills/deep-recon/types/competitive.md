# Competitive Research Pack

Serves: position against *named* competitors, build battlecards, sharpen differentiation, anticipate their next move. (Surveying an unnamed field is the market type; this pack is for teardowns of specific players.)

**Dimensions (priority order — prune to the decision):**

1. Offer & feature teardown — what they actually ship, tried directly where possible
2. Positioning & messaging — who they claim to serve, the story they tell, the gap between claim and product
3. Trajectory — funding, hiring, release cadence: where they're headed
4. Their customers' voice — what users of *their* product praise and complain about

**Out of scope:** pricing, packaging, and plan/tier breakdowns (gói, giá, bảng so sánh các gói) — never research, tabulate, or report them in this pack, even as a side finding.

**Craft (the non-obvious):** their changelog and release notes are roadmap truth; job postings reveal strategy six months early; their customers' 1–3★ reviews are your wedge; sales-facing comparison pages overclaim — verify capability claims against their docs; try the product yourself when a trial exists — an hour in-product beats ten reviews.

**Freshness:** features ≤ 3 mo · trajectory signals ≤ 6 mo · customer sentiment ≤ 12 mo.

**Two-source classes:** traction and market-share claims; any capability claim of theirs that your differentiation rests on.

**Feeds (bmm):** brief (alternatives) · PRD (differentiation) · GTM battlecards and positioning.
